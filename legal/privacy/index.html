Legal

Privacy policy.

What we collect, why we collect it, and who is allowed to see it.

(01)Status

The full policy is published here before Smart Start opens on 15 October 2026, after review by counsel. The practices below are settled, and are what that policy will document.

(02)What is settled

  • Your identity documents and your credit report are stored isolated to your file, encrypted at rest, and never served from a public path.
  • They are seen by Smart Start and by the partner organisation working on your file. Nobody else.
  • Your Social Security card is held encrypted and is never shown back to you in full.
  • Your consent is recorded with the version of the document you accepted and the time you accepted it, and you can see that record inside your account.
  • We do not connect to your bank accounts, and we will never ask you for a banking password.
  • The free assessment asks for no Social Security number and no card.
