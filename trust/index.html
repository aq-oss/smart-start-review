Trust and disclosures

What Smart Start is, and is not.

Plain language, in the open, where you can read it before you decide anything. No small print, because there is nothing here we would rather you missed.

(01)The honest question

Is credit repair worth paying for?

We are not going to tell you yes. We are going to give you the test to apply, including to us.

What you can do yourself, for nothing

You can obtain your credit report and dispute anything inaccurate on it directly with the credit bureau, yourself, free of charge. That right is written into the Fair Credit Reporting Act. If your only problem is one obvious error, you may not need anybody at all.

What a paid service legally may and may not do

The Credit Repair Organizations Act sets the rules. A credit repair organisation may not take payment before the service has been performed. It must give you a written statement of your rights before you sign. It must give you three business days to cancel. And it may not make an untrue or misleading statement about what it can achieve for you.

Anyone promising a specific score, a specific number of points, or the removal of accurate information is telling you something the law does not permit them to tell you.

How Smart Start is different

We start with readiness rather than with repair. You get the assessment before you have an account and before any money is discussed. You set a goal with a real date and we tell you plainly whether that date works. The work on the file is carried out by an attorney affiliated partner organisation, and every stage of it is visible to you as it moves.

The test to apply

Ask any service four things. What exactly will you do. What will it cost and when do I pay. What happens if it does not work. And what are you promising me in writing. A service that will not answer the fourth question plainly has told you everything you need to know.

(03)Your rights

What the law gives you, whoever you use.

Before you sign anything with a credit repair organisation, federal law requires that you be given a separate written statement of your rights under the Credit Repair Organizations Act, 15 U.S.C. 1679 and following. Read it. It is not a formality.

Under the same Act, a credit repair organisation may not charge or receive money for a service before that service has been fully performed.

Federal law gives you three business days to cancel a credit repair contract, without paying anything, from the date you sign it.

Under the Fair Credit Reporting Act you may dispute anything on your credit report directly with the credit bureau, yourself, at no cost. The bureau then has a statutory reinvestigation window, set by 15 U.S.C. 1681i, in which to respond. You do not have to pay anyone to exercise that right.

This is a plain language summary. The statutory statement of rights is given to you in full and separately, as the Act requires.
