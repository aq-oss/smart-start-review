Readiness

No. It is Smart Start’s own read of where you stand across income, obligations, debt, savings and your goal. It does not come from a credit bureau, it is not a FICO or VantageScore number, and no lender will ever see it.

It depends on what is actually on your file. An error corrected can change things within a reporting cycle. The length of your credit history only time changes. Disputes run on the credit bureaus’ statutory clock. We do not publish a timeline, because any timeline we published would be a guess dressed up as a promise.

Credit

Often yes, and usually expensively. Lenders set their own cut offs, and at lower bands the rate, the term and the deposit all move against you. The question worth asking first is what is holding your file at that level, and whether it is one item or several.

Disputing inaccurate information works, because the law requires the credit bureau to reinvestigate it. Removing accurate, properly reported information does not work, and anybody who says otherwise is describing something they are not permitted to promise.

You can dispute errors yourself, free of charge. What a service should be offering you is the work, the order to do it in, and the visibility, not a miracle.

Credit repair concerns what is on your credit report and whether it is accurate. Credit counseling concerns your budget and your debts, and often involves a repayment plan agreed with your creditors. They answer different questions, and one is not a substitute for the other.

You see your readiness band, the single largest obstacle in your way, and a verdict on whether your goal is reachable as things stand. From there you can create an account and set a goal with a real date, or take the answer and do nothing at all. Both are fine.

The work

You provide the documents and upload what arrives. Smart Start assesses your readiness, reads your report back to you plainly and shows you every stage. The strategy, the disputes, the creditor contact and the evaluation of what comes back are carried out by an attorney affiliated partner organisation.

Three pieces of identification, a driver’s license, a Social Security card and a utility bill, and then your credit report. You upload each once.

Money

Pricing is published in full before Smart Start opens on 15 October 2026. What is settled today is that the assessment is free, that no card is required to create an account, and that federal law forbids a credit repair organisation from charging for a service before it has been performed.

Cancellation and refund terms are published in full before we open. Separately, federal law gives you three business days to cancel a credit repair contract after signing it, at no cost.
